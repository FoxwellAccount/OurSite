Welcome to Foxwell Games

Foxwell Collective Interactive, publicly known as Foxwell Games, is an independent game development studio dedicated to crafting deeply immersive, psychologically challenging, and narratively complex experiences.

The studio's name originates directly from the middle name of our very first consolidated character, Fizzy Foxwell, standing as a permanent tribute to our roots.

Projects

Institute Of Everlight: Relics of the Golden Age

Status: In Development

Officially born as an institute setting on October 14, 2025.

Technical Sheet

  • Engine: Unity Engine URP
  • Target Audience: Teens (14+)
  • Genres: Analog Horror, 2000s Aesthetic, Mascot Horror, Physical Puzzles

Premise (Spoiler-Free)

A proposal is received from Paragon Asset Recovery to collect valuable items and lost reports inside the abandoned Institute in exchange for high compensation. The contractor must navigate the decaying infrastructure, utilizing analog tools, and fulfill high-risk objectives left behind from a forgotten era before the facility reclaims what is left.

Foxwell Launcher (FL)

Status: Planned

The official studio launcher designed to seamlessly handle user progress, cross-platform scoring, and unlockable DLCs through daily objectives. It will feature robust cloud data synchronization bridging the gap between Steam and Epic Games ecosystems. FL will be natively integrated and fully utilized for Institute Of Everlight.

Foxwell Systems (FS)

Status: In Development

An expansive proprietary collection and organized framework of scripts and systems designed to severely accelerate project development. It includes highly advanced visual systems capable of performing specific logical operations without requiring direct code programming from the artists. FS will be the architectural backbone utilized in Institute Of Everlight.

About Projects Evolution

Institute Of Everlight

Development Curiosity: The game's original conceptual setting was a twisted circus environment. On October 14, 2025, the entire scenario shifted to a corporate/scientific institute, giving birth to the current trajectory of the project.

Lore Curiosity: The foundational base of the current narrative lore was successfully consolidated on November 18, 2025, though it continuously undergoes micro-refinements.

Character Origins

Fizzy Foxwell

Fizzy existed as a raw concept for a circus stuffed animal since early 2025. Originally, he was drafted as a circus bear named simply "Tommy". He later received his definitive anatomical fox design. The final iteration of a circus fox was locked in on September 23, 2025. The origin of Fizzy as a circus entity is intrinsically tied to the fact that the project's original discarded setting was a circus.

Clarity Glasswell

She was originally formulated as a seasonal New Year character. Clarity was engineered in late December, specifically on December 28, 2025. Although she was released on the planned date and exact time, intense artistic doubts arose regarding her initial design metrics. After a considerable period of re-evaluation, completely new designs were authored and are now officially documented in the system blogs.
